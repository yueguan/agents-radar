### 3. Trend Signal Analysis

The most explosive community attention is going to agent operations layers rather than new foundation models. Today’s top AI trending gains are design/skills and harness projects—[pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) (+2,881), [HKUDS/DeepTutor](https://github.com/HKUDS/DeepTutor) (+1,926), [milind-soni/OpenMausBot](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot) (+1,190), [elayadesign/ai-design-skills](https://github.com/elayadesign/ai-design-skills) (+1,178), [JetBrains/thinkrail](https://github.com/JetBrains/thinkrail) (+1,116) and [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) (+1,037)—plus local-first teammate workspaces like [nightly-labs/openbot](https://github.com/nightly-labs/openbot). This indicates teams are standardizing how agents are steered, audited, given tools, and made persistent, not just which LLM they call.

The new stack is visibly TypeScript-heavy for desktop/agent workspaces and React/AG-UI front ends ([CopilotKit/CopilotKit](https://github.com/CopilotKit/CopilotKit), [nightly-labs/openbot](https://github.com/nightly-labs/openbot), [CherryHQ/cherry-studio](https://github.com/CherryHQ/cherry-studio)), while Rust appears in performance-sensitive or IDE-adjacent tooling ([pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), [codewhale-hq/Codewhale](https://github.com/codewhale-hq/Codewhale), [0xPlaygrounds/rig](https://github.com/0xPlaygrounds/rig)). Shell-based skills frameworks show a packaging layer forming around Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and similar coding agents.

Memory and context are also separating into dedicated infrastructure: [thedotmack/claude-mem](https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem), [mem0ai/mem0](https://github.com/mem0ai/mem0), [headroomlabs-ai/headroom](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom), [volcengine/OpenViking](https://github.com/volcengine/OpenViking) and [VectifyAI/PageIndex](https://github.com/VectifyAI/PageIndex) point to compression, persistent session memory, context databases and vectorless reasoning-based retrieval. This is a direct response to longer agent sessions, tool-output bloat, and the cost of repeatedly feeding context to models. The model-runner layer remains important—[ollama/ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama)’s support for Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, DeepSeek, gpt-oss, Qwen and Gemma keeps local-first workflows viable—but differentiation is shifting upward to orchestration, memory and vertical workflow packaging. Enterprise low-code and vertical agents ([jeecgboot/JeecgBoot](https://github.com/jeecgboot/JeecgBoot), [career-ops-hq/career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops), [ZhuLinsen/daily_stock_analysis](https://github.com/ZhuLinsen/daily_stock_analysis), [hugohe3/ppt-master](https://github.com/hugohe3/ppt-master)) show agent patterns moving from demos into packaged, repeatable business tasks.
